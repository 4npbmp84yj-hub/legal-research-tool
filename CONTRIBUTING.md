# Contributing to the Indian Legal Research Toolkit

Thank you for contributing.

This project aims to provide an organised, transparent, and useful open-source framework for legal research in India.

## What You Can Contribute

Contributions may include:

- Legal research resources
- Research templates
- Corrections to existing material
- Documentation improvements
- Publicly available legal resources
- Research methodology improvements
- Case-law research notes
- Statutory research notes
- Suggestions for improving the project

## Before Contributing

Please:

1. Check whether the information is already present.
2. Verify legal authorities against reliable primary sources.
3. Provide citations where appropriate.
4. Avoid copying copyrighted material.
5. Clearly distinguish legal analysis from the underlying authority.
6. Do not submit confidential or personal information.

## Case Research Contributions

When submitting research concerning a judgment, include:

- Case name
- Court
- Citation
- Date
- Legal issue
- Relevant provisions
- Holding
- Ratio decidendi
- Relevant paragraph numbers
- Subsequent treatment
- Sources

## Statutory Contributions

When submitting statutory research, identify:

- Act
- Year
- Section
- Relevant amendments
- Commencement
- Rules or regulations
- Important judicial interpretation
- Sources

## Accuracy

Contributors should make reasonable efforts to verify legal information.

Legal information can change through legislation, amendments, regulations, and judicial decisions. Contributors should indicate when information was last verified where appropriate.

## Pull Requests

A pull request should explain:

- What was changed
- Why it was changed
- Which sources were consulted
- Any limitations or uncertainties

## Issues

Use issues to report:

- Incorrect information
- Broken links
- Missing authorities
- Outdated information
- Suggested improvements

## Disclaimer

This project is an educational and research resource. Contributions do not constitute legal advice.
