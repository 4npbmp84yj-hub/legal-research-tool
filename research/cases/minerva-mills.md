# Minerva Mills Ltd. v. Union of India

## Case Identification

**Court:** Supreme Court of India

**Year:** 1980

**Citation:** (1980) 3 SCC 625

## Legal Issue

Whether constitutional amendments can destroy the basic structure of the Constitution.

## Decision

The Supreme Court applied the basic structure doctrine and held that Parliament's amending power is subject to constitutional limitations.

## Key Principle

Parliament cannot use its power of constitutional amendment to destroy the basic structure of the Constitution.
