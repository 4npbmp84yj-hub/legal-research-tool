# I.R. Coelho v. State of Tamil Nadu

## Case Identification

**Court:** Supreme Court of India

**Year:** 2007

**Citation:** (2007) 2 SCC 1

## Legal Issue

Whether laws placed in the Ninth Schedule of the Constitution can be completely protected from judicial review.

## Decision

The Supreme Court held that constitutional amendments placing laws in the Ninth Schedule after 24 April 1973 can be examined where they damage the basic structure of the Constitution.

## Key Principle

The basic structure doctrine limits the power to amend the Constitution, including amendments affecting the Ninth Schedule.
