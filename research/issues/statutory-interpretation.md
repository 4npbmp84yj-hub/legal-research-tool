# Statutory Interpretation

## Purpose

Research on how courts interpret constitutional provisions, statutes, rules, and other legal texts.

## Principal Approaches

- Literal interpretation
- Golden rule
- Mischief rule
- Purposive interpretation
- Harmonious construction
- Beneficial construction
- Strict construction
- Liberal construction

## Interpretive Principles

- Ejusdem generis
- Noscitur a sociis
- Expressio unius est exclusio alterius
- Generalia specialibus non derogant
- Ut res magis valeat quam pereat
- Casus omissus
- Presumption against retrospectivity

## Key Legal Questions

- What is the ordinary meaning of the statutory text?
- Is the language ambiguous?
- What is the legislative purpose?
- What is the relationship between conflicting provisions?
- Does the provision operate retrospectively?
- Does a proviso qualify the main provision?
- How should an exception be interpreted?
- What material can be used to determine legislative purpose?
