# Legal Proposition Index

This index connects specific legal propositions with the authorities supporting them.

## Constitutional Law

### Basic Structure Doctrine

**Proposition:** Parliament's power to amend the Constitution under Article 368 is subject to limitations arising from the Constitution's basic structure.

**Primary Authority:**

- *Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala*, (1973) 4 SCC 225

**Case Note:** [Kesavananda Bharati](./cases/kesavananda-bharati.md)

---

### Articles 14, 19 and 21

**Proposition:** Constitutional scrutiny of laws affecting personal liberty involves consideration of the guarantees contained in Articles 14, 19 and 21.

**Primary Authority:**

- *Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India*, (1978) 1 SCC 248

**Case Note:** [Maneka Gandhi](./cases/maneka-gandhi.md)

## How to Add a Proposition

Each entry should contain:

1. The precise legal proposition.
2. The primary authority supporting it.
3. The relevant constitutional or statutory provision.
4. A link to the detailed case note.
5. Subsequent authorities, where relevant.
6. A verification date.

Avoid stating broad legal conclusions when the authority supports only a narrower proposition.
