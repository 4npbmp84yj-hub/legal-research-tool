# Legal Research Sources

## Primary Sources

- Constitution of India
- Central and State legislation
- Rules and regulations
- Ordinances
- Notifications
- Government orders
- Judicial decisions

## Indian Legal Databases

- Supreme Court of India
- eCourts
- India Code
- Legislative Department
- High Courts
- Official government portals

## Secondary Sources

- Law journals
- Legal textbooks
- Commentaries
- Research papers
- Law Commission reports
- Parliamentary materials

## Research Principles

Always prefer primary and authoritative sources where available.

Verify the current status of legislation and judicial decisions before relying on them.

Record the source, date accessed, citation, and relevant proposition when conducting legal research.
